Your Journey — Step by Step

Restoration Before Release

Every step is intentional. Every module builds on the last. This is not a program — it is a pathway to your destiny.

  1. 01
    Module 1 & 2 · Nine Weeks

    Encounter with God

    Focus: Healing, freedom, and breaking the power of lies.

    Before you can walk in authority, you must be free. This encounter module leads you through deep inner healing, breaking off every bondage brought into your Christian walk. Hidden wounds are surfaced, lies are broken at the root, and the foundation of your identity in Christ is firmly established.

    • Healed and set free from bondages
    • Lies of the enemy exposed and broken
    • Identity anchored in God's love
  2. 02
    Module 3 · Nine Weeks

    Biblical Foundations

    Focus: Character and spiritual maturity.

    With your identity secured, this module plants you firmly in God's Word. You will build unshakeable doctrinal clarity, understand God's nature and character, and grow a biblical worldview that governs every decision — in business, family, and community.

    • Grounded in God's Word
    • Doctrinal clarity established
    • Biblical worldview formed
  3. 03
    Module 4 · Nine Weeks

    7 Mountain Mandate Identification

    Focus: Discovering your calling and sphere of influence.

    God has placed you in a specific mountain of influence. In this module you will identify your mountain — whether Religion, Family, Education, Government, Media, Business, or Arts & Entertainment — and understand how your gifts, experiences, and calling align with your God-given assignment as a societal reformer.

    • Destiny and calling clarified
    • Sphere of influence identified
    • 7 Mountain assignment understood
  4. 04
    Encounter Weekend · Graduation

    Servant Leadership

    Focus: Transitioning from learner to leader.

    The final module marks the transition. You are no longer a student — you are equipped to lead. You will understand what it means to serve your mountain of influence and are prepared to open your own New Christian Training Centre or begin coaching others through the same pathway you have walked.

    • Ready to disciple others
    • Equipped to lead in your sphere
    • Pathway complete — legacy begins

What You Will Become

Every Disciple Who Walks This Path Becomes

"From pagan background to societal reformer with a biblical worldview, in 12 months or less."

  • 01

    Healed & Set Free

    Free from every bondage, wound, and lie brought into your Christian walk. No past is too broken for God's restoration.

  • 02

    Grounded in God's Word

    Strong doctrinal clarity and a deep understanding of Scripture as the standard for every area of life.

  • 03

    Secure Identity in Christ

    You know who you are, who God is, and where you stand. Identity leads to authority. Authority leads to responsibility.

  • 04

    Biblical Worldview Established

    The Bible becomes your standard and code of conduct — in business, workplace, schools, and personal life.

  • 05

    Destiny & Calling Clarified

    Your mountain of influence is identified. You understand your God-given sphere and the assignment written over your life.

  • 06

    Ready to Disciple Others

    You finish as a leader equipped to take others through the same pathway — multiplying the movement one disciple at a time.
