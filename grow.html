The Great Melbourne Revival of 1902 and the Welsh Revival of 1905 ignited a powerful transformation across entire cities by restoring families, reducing crime, and leading thousands to Christ.

Yet time and again, without a solid discipleship framework to sustain the harvest, revival fades, and the world crept back in.

Now, with the prophesied “billion soul harvest” on the horizon (Amos 3:7), the urgency has never been greater. We cannot afford to repeat the mistakes of the past.

We must rise with a clear, structured discipleship plan to sustain what God is about to do (Matt. 12:44-45).

Growing Deep and Strong® exists to change that. Our world-class, trialed, and tested discipleship system offers a 19-week Discipleship Program and an 18-week Leadership Program, designed to equip believers with a biblical worldview.

Highly scalable and adaptable to all learning styles, it empowers individuals to grow in faith, disciple nations, and create lasting transformation.
