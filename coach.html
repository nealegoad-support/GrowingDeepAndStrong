Your Coaching System

The 4-Phase Discipleship Pathway

Each phase is a complete, ready-to-deploy module. Walk one person or a small group through all four and watch ordinary believers become extraordinary reformers.

  1. 01

    Prayer Strategy

    Opens the heavens

    Before anything else, prayer opens the heavens over your disciples' lives. This phase equips your group with a clear prayer strategy — interceding for breakthrough, protection, and divine connection for those they will reach.

    • Intercessory prayer framework established
    • Spiritual atmosphere prepared
    • Heavens opened over your sphere
  2. 02

    Evangelism & Salvation

    Harvesting

    With the heavens open, you go to harvest. This phase equips you and your group to lead others to salvation with confidence — understanding the gospel, how to share it naturally, and how to bring people into the family of God.

    • Confident gospel presentation
    • Natural evangelism in everyday life
    • New converts welcomed and grounded
  3. 03

    Find Your Destiny

    Encounter, identity & healing

    This is where transformation happens at the root. You walk your disciples through deep inner healing, break the power of lies and wounds, and anchor their identity in Christ — establishing the foundation everything else is built on.

    • Identity anchored in God's love
    • Bondages broken at the root
    • Encounter with God — not just knowledge
  4. 04

    Growing Deep and Strong

    Biblical foundations & maturity

    The final phase plants disciples firmly in God's Word, builds doctrinal clarity, and develops the biblical worldview that will govern their lives as societal reformers. Graduates are ready to open their own training centre and coach others.

    • Unshakeable doctrinal clarity
    • Biblical worldview formed
    • Ready to multiply — disciple others

  • II

    Family
