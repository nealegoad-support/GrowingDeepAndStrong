Restoration Before Release

The Curriculum Structure

Three sequential phases — each building on the last — designed so that a healed, grounded disciple becomes a released, effective societal reformer.

  1. I
    Phase 1

    Find Your Destiny

    Encounter with God
    9 Modules Evangelism Video 2 Dedicated Weekends

    Focus: Shifting from bondage to "Who Am I?" — healing identity, breaking lies, and awakening divine purpose.

    Before you can walk in authority, you must know who you are. This phase takes you deep into the encounter — inner healing, deliverance, and a personal revelation of God's love that exposes and breaks every lie that has shaped your identity. You emerge knowing your name: beloved, chosen, and sent.

    • Healed of identity wounds and past trauma
    • Bondages broken at the root
    • Divine purpose awakened
  2. II
    Phase 2

    Growing Deep and Strong

    Biblical Foundations
    19-Week Track 37-Week Track Nine Core Modules

    Focus: Establishing strong biblical foundations, building Christ-like character, and cultivating deep spiritual maturity.

    With identity secured, this phase plants you firmly in God's Word. Choose the 19-week accelerated track or the comprehensive 37-week journey — both built on nine core modules developing sound doctrine, Christ-like character, and the spiritual maturity needed to walk as a Kingdom citizen in every area of life.

    • Unshakeable doctrinal clarity
    • Biblical worldview governing all decisions
    • Character shaped by God's Word
  3. III
    Phase 3

    Sent

    The Societal Reformer Journey
    Marketplace Ministry Kingdom Assignment

    Focus: Mountain identification, understanding your specific sphere of influence, and walking in authority through servant leadership.

    With identity rooted and the Word living in you, it is time to move. This final phase positions you in your mountain — the specific sphere of cultural influence God has assigned to you. You will understand your calling, walk in servant leadership, and begin reforming your corner of society.

    • Mountain of influence identified
    • Servant leadership activated
    • Societal reformation begins

Identity → Foundation → Release  ·  From pagan background to societal reformer — in 12 months or less.

Where Disciples Become Reformers

Spheres of Societal Reformation

Establishing a biblical worldview to carry Christ into every pillar of society.

  • I

    Religion

    The Church as a discipleship factory — raising reformers, not filling seats.

  • II

    Family

    Covenant families as the primary unit of discipleship and societal health.

  • III

    Education

    Biblical truth in schools and universities — shaping the next generation's worldview.

  • IV

    Government

    Righteous governance built on God's law — justice and servant leadership in office.

  • V

    Media

    Truth-telling and Kingdom narratives in journalism, broadcasting, and digital platforms.

  • VI

    Business

    Kingdom economics — ethical enterprise that funds mission and serves community.

  • VII

    Arts & Entertainment

    Culture-shaping creativity that reflects God's glory and tells redemptive stories.
