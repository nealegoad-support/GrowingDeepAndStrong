Why Moves of God Succeeded and Faded

A study of the repeating cycle of rebellion and renewal — why revival rises in fire, why it so often fades to ash, and the apostolic blueprint that turns a momentary awakening into a movement that reforms nations.

Jeremiah 17:7 and 8

Throughout history, nations and civilisations have repeatedly followed a predictable spiritual cycle. Societies prosper, turn to moral decay, forsake God, and inevitably face internal disintegration — until a faithful remnant cries out, and divine intervention brings renewal. This pattern of decline, judgement, and revival is not confined to biblical Israel. It is visible in the rise and fall of empires, the shaping of Western civilisation, and even in the most recent moves of God.

The Purpose of This Document

This document presents key historical periods to illustrate the repeating cycle of rebellion and renewal. Each section highlights the moral decay and its societal effects, God's intervention and the immediate results of revival, the long-term legacy and transformation, and the importance of an apostolic blueprint for lasting change.

At its core are two engines: Growing Deep and Strong®, which grounds believers in unshakable discipleship, and Find Your Destiny™, which activates them into their God-given purpose to influence every sphere of culture. Together they form a scalable framework that ensures revival doesn't end as a moment — but becomes a movement that shapes nations.

But history warns us: revival without discipleship fades.

The State of Our Society Today — A Tipping Point

We now live in a godless Western society that has systematically removed God from the centre of life. Family breakdown, identity confusion, and sexual immorality mirror the days of Noah and Rome before its fall. Generational drift from faith has produced the rise of the “nones” — young adults with no anchor in God or Scripture. Mental-health crises, social polarisation, and moral relativism are the inevitable fruit of a culture that crowns man as his own god.

We are standing at a historic tipping point: either society continues its decline into chaos and darkness, or a sovereign move of God reshapes our world again.

Why Moves of God Fade

Even the most powerful revivals diminish when spiritual vigilance weakens — through complacency after blessing, shallow discipleship, pride and man-centred control, failure to transfer revival to the next generation, worldly allure, and the decline of prayer and repentance.

Revival is not meant to be an event but a sustained lifestyle of holiness, prayer, and discipleship. When a move becomes memory instead of motion, society inevitably drifts back into darkness.

The Repeating Cycle Across History

Eight post-biblical moments where moral decay met divine intervention — each rising in fire, each leaving a legacy, many fading once the discipleship that sustained them was neglected.

  1. 01
    3rd–5th Century AD

    The Fall of Rome & the Rise of Christendom

    Rome rotted from within — hedonism, devaluation of life, and government corruption. The rise of Christianity closed pagan temples, abolished the gladiator games, outlawed infanticide, and birthed the first hospitals, orphanages, and organised social care. Christian moral law became the foundation of Western civilisation.

  2. 02
    8th–9th Century AD

    The Dark Ages & the Carolingian Renewal

    After Rome's collapse, Europe fell into lawlessness and biblical ignorance. Charlemagne's coronation (AD 800) revived Christian education and re-established biblical law, while Benedictine monasticism preserved Scripture, trained leaders, and re-evangelised Europe step by step.

  3. 03
    16th Century

    The Protestant Reformation

    Against indulgences, simony, and Scripture kept from the people, Luther, Calvin, and Zwingli called the Church back to grace and the Word. Mass literacy followed the desire to read the Bible; the Protestant work ethic and constitutional government grew from a biblical worldview of responsibility under God.

  4. 04
    1730s–1750s

    The First Great Awakening

    Edwards, Whitefield, and Wesley preached repentance and new birth into a cold, formal church. Taverns and theatres emptied, crime fell, and the seeds of modern missions — and of biblical principles of equality and liberty — were sown.

  5. 05
    1790s–1840s

    The Second Great Awakening

    Finney, Cartwright, and the camp meetings transformed whole frontier towns — saloons closed and jails emptied. It fuelled abolition, temperance, women's education, and a wave of Christian-founded universities.

  6. 06
    1904–1905

    The Welsh Revival

    Evan Roberts and youth prayer groups ignited a national revival — 100,000 conversions in one year (5% of the population). Crime dropped so sharply that police formed choirs, courts sat empty, and bars closed for lack of customers.

  7. 07
    1906–1915

    The Azusa Street Revival

    In a segregated America, a fresh outpouring of the Spirit broke racial, social, and gender barriers under the humble leadership of William Seymour. Its missionary fire birthed the global Pentecostal and Charismatic movement — over 700 million believers today trace their roots to it.

  8. 08
    Late 1960s–1970s

    The Jesus People Movement

    Into the sexual revolution, drug epidemic, and spiritual vacuum of the counterculture, thousands of hippies encountered Jesus. Mass ocean baptisms, the birth of contemporary Christian music, and the Calvary Chapel and Vineyard movements revitalised evangelical Christianity — yet many converts drifted back into the materialism of the 1980s.

The Critical Gap in Past Moves of God

Revivals ignite but fizzle for three recurring reasons:

  • Lack of deep discipleship — converts feel an emotional encounter but are never rooted in Word and Spirit. Shallow faith, quick backsliding.
  • No systematic equipping — churches lean on charismatic leadership instead of multiplying mature disciples. The fire fades with its generation.
  • No societal integration — revival stays inside church walls rather than equipping marketplace reformers. Society returns to secular control once the wave passes.

Why Bible Colleges & the Church Are Failing

When Bible colleges prioritise information over transformation, institutional survival over missional fruit, and accreditation over activation, they produce graduates who are theologically informed but spiritually underdeveloped — unequipped to disciple others in a way that sustains revival. The current church model often creates consumers of religious goods rather than Kingdom reformers.

The Facts — American Worldview Inventory 2022

Cultural Research Centre, Arizona Christian University (George Barna, Director of Research) — survey of ~1,000 Christian pastors.

  • 37%of all pastors hold a biblical worldview — 62% are syncretistic
  • 12%of children's & youth pastors hold a biblical worldview
  • 13%of teaching pastors hold a biblical worldview
  • 4%of executive pastors hold a biblical worldview

Because worldview is largely formed before age 13, the fact that 7 of 8 children's and youth pastors lack a biblical worldview may be a key reason so few young people develop one.

Returning to the Blueprint

For nearly three centuries the New Testament church flourished without state support — a Spirit-led, relational movement meeting in homes, rooted in the apostles' teaching, reproducing leaders, and integrating worship, fellowship, prayer, and mission into daily life. The task before us is not to replicate their culture, but to restore their priorities.

By reclaiming relational discipleship, participatory gatherings, Spirit-led leadership, and mission beyond church walls, we can again embody the Kingdom culture that turned the world upside down.

The Apostolic Blueprint — Five Movements

The strategy that ensures the coming awakening does not fade: deep discipleship, systematic equipping, and societal integration that multiplies across generations.

  • 01

    Root in the Word & Spirit

    Move converts beyond an emotional encounter into unshakable discipleship grounded in Scripture, prayer, and holiness — so faith survives the cooling of the fire.

  • 02

    Equip Systematically

    Replace dependence on charismatic leadership with a reproducible, off-the-shelf curriculum that matures every believer rather than a select few.

  • 03

    Activate Destiny

    Through Find Your Destiny™, send believers into their God-given sphere — the seven mountains of culture — as societal reformers carrying a biblical worldview.

  • 04

    Integrate with Society

    Take discipleship beyond church walls into business, education, media, government, and the arts — so transformation outlasts the emotional wave.

  • 05

    Multiply Across Generations

    Disciples who make disciples. Institutionalise revival in family and relational discipleship so the torch passes — and the movement multiplies until Christ returns.

Who We're Looking For

We are looking for hungry believers, marketplace leaders, and would-be disciple-makers who refuse to let the next move of God fade — those ready to be rooted deeply, sent into their sphere of influence, and equipped to raise societal reformers in their own homes, workplaces, and communities.
